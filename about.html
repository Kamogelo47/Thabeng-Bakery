About Thabeng Bakery

Thabeng Bakery is a small fictional bakery based in Johannesburg, South Africa. The bakery was established to provide fresh, affordable and high-quality baked products to individuals, families and local businesses.

Our products include freshly baked bread, cakes, cupcakes, pastries and customised celebration cakes.

Our Mission

To provide customers with fresh, affordable and high-quality baked products while delivering friendly and reliable customer service.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local bakery recognised for quality products, creative designs and excellent customer experiences.
